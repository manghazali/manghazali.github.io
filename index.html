About

I'm a software engineer with 8+ years of experience in fintech systems, payment gateway integration, enterprise applications and backend web development. I build scalable backend systems, REST APIs, payment processing platforms and third-party integrations with PHP, Laravel, Java and SQL.

My work spans payment gateways, FPX and card integrations, Malaysia e-Invoice, settlement H2H processing, and government systems for the Ministry of Finance and PTPTN. I work in agile teams and I'm comfortable with the whole lifecycle, from requirements and architecture to UAT, release and production monitoring.
